About KEYSTROKE

A tactile 3D tribute to custom mechanical keyboards.

Inspired by collectible industrial design and warm editorial illustration, KEYSTROKE pairs a procedural Three.js 3D mechanical keyboard with a real-time Web Audio switch synthesizer.

How it works: Every physical key press (`keydown` and `keyup` via `KeyboardEvent.code`) depresses the matching sculpted 3D keycap with spring physics, triggers a custom-filtered acoustic transient ("thock" for spacebar and modifiers, crisp "clack" for alphas), and measures your net WPM, accuracy, and rhythm consistency.

Interactive Knob: Click or drag the cream rotary encoder knob on the front-left side of the 3D keyboard case to shift the acoustic pitch profile of the switches.
